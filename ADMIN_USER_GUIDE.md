## 2. Managing Products

All products created in the Admin Panel automatically sync in **real-time** to your live website at **[nihistudio.com](https://www.nihistudio.com)**.

---

### A. Adding a New Product

1. On the left sidebar menu, navigate to **Products** → **Add New**.
2. **Product Name** *(Top Box)*: Enter the title of your jewelry item (e.g., `Aura Solitaire Diamond Ring`).
3. **Product Description**: Type detailed product craftsmanship, metal purity, and care instructions in the main editor box.
4. **Product Pricing** *(Scroll down to "Product data" → General)*:
   - **Regular price (₹)**: Set the standard retail price in INR (e.g., `2999`).
   - **Sale price (₹)** *(Optional)*: Set a discounted price (e.g., `2499`).
5. **Product Main Image** *(Right Sidebar)*:
   - Under **Product image**, click **Set product image**.
   - Upload a high-resolution, square photo of the item.
6. **Product Gallery Images** *(Right Sidebar)*:
   - Click **Add product gallery images** to upload additional views or model shots.
7. **Assign Category** *(Right Sidebar)*:
   - Under **Product categories**, check the category box (e.g., `Rings`, `Earrings`, `Necklaces`, `Bracelets`, `Gifts`).
8. **Publish**:
   - Click the blue **Publish** button on the top-right panel. Your product is now **LIVE** on `nihistudio.com`!

---

### B. Editing an Existing Product

1. Go to **Products** → **All Products**.
2. Hover over the product you want to edit and click **Edit**.
3. Update any information (title, price, image, description, category).
4. Click the blue **Update** button on the right panel to save changes.

---

### C. Deleting a Product

1. Go to **Products** → **All Products**.
2. **Delete Single Product**: Hover over the product title and click **Trash** (red text).
3. **Delete Multiple Products**: Check the boxes next to the items → Click **Bulk actions** at the top → Select **Move to Trash** → Click **Apply**.

---

### D. Understanding SKUs & Stock

- **What is an SKU?**: **Stock Keeping Unit** is a unique identification code for warehouse tracking (e.g., `NIHI-RNG-01`).
- **Is SKU Mandatory?**: **No, it is 100% Optional**. If left blank, the website automatically generates an internal tracking ID.
- **Stock Status**: Under *Product Data → Inventory*, set Stock Status to `In stock` or `Out of stock`.

## 3. Managing Categories & Homepage Strips

Categories organize your store navigation and populate the **Circular Category Strip** on the homepage.

### Adding or Updating a Category:
1. Go to **Products** → **Categories**.
2. Type the **Name** (e.g., `Silver 925`, `Gifts`, `Bracelets`).
3. **Upload Thumbnail Image**: Scroll down to **Thumbnail** and click **Upload/Add image**. This image will display as the circular icon on the homepage strip!

## 4. Order Management & Processing

Whenever a customer places an order on **[nihistudio.com](https://www.nihistudio.com)**, the order is recorded in your admin panel.

### Viewing Incoming Orders:
1. Navigate to **WooCommerce** → **Orders**.
2. Click on any Order Number (e.g., `#1042`) to view:
   - Customer Name, Email, and Mobile Phone.
   - Delivery Address (House No, Street, City, State, Pincode).
   - Purchased Jewelry Items, Selected Ring Sizes, and Custom Laser Engraving Requests.
   - Payment Status (Paid via Razorpay UPI / Card / NetBanking).

### Updating Order Status:
- **Processing**: Order received and payment confirmed. Item is being packed.
- **Completed**: Item handed over to courier / delivered to customer.
- **Cancelled / Refunded**: Order cancelled.

## 5. Payment Verification (Razorpay)

All online payments are processed securely via **Razorpay**.

1. Log into your **Razorpay Dashboard**: **[dashboard.razorpay.com](https://dashboard.razorpay.com)**
2. Click **Payments** to view live transactions, UPI payments, and settlements to your bank account.
3. Every order in WooCommerce automatically tags the corresponding **Razorpay Payment ID** for easy audit.

## 6. Courier Shipping & Tracking (Shiprocket)

Insured express shipping and tracking numbers are handled via **Shiprocket**.

1. Log into your **Shiprocket Dashboard**: **[app.shiprocket.in](https://app.shiprocket.in)**
2. Incoming orders automatically sync to Shiprocket.
3. Click **Generate AWB / Label** to print shipping labels with customer address barcodes.
4. Once picked up, tracking updates are sent to the customer via SMS & Email.

## 7. Customer Service & Engraving Requests

Nihi Studio offers **Free Laser Engraving** and **Luxury Velvet Gifting**.
